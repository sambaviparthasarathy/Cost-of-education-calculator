About this data

NSS 80th Round — Comprehensive Modular Survey: Education, 2025 (NSO/MoSPI, April–June 2025). Covers household out-of-pocket school expenditure across India.

UDISE+ — Unified District Information System for Education Plus. Annual expenditure per reported student by school type, level, and state.
